Honest ATS guidance, not fear

The "75% of resumes are auto-rejected by robots" line is a myth. An ATS is a searchable database recruiters use to store and find candidates — not a gatekeeper that throws you out. What actually hurts you is a layout that parses badly (multi-column, tables, text in images) and missing the keywords a recruiter searches for. Our ATS resume checker fixes both: it guarantees a parse-safe structure and tells you exactly which real skills to add — never keyword-stuffing, which the human reviewer sees right through.
