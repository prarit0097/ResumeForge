The free AI resume builder that actually gets you found

ResumeForge is a free, no-login resume builder and ATS checker. Start from scratch and let AI write strong, quantified bullet points, or upload an existing PDF or Word resume and we'll restructure it to be ATS-friendly in seconds. Either way you get a live ATS resume score, one-click tailoring to any job description, and a clean file you can download for free.

How it works

  1. Build or upload. Answer a few prompts, or upload your current resume to enhance it.
  2. Check your ATS score. Get a 0–100 compatibility score with specific, actionable fixes.
  3. Tailor to the job. Paste a job description to see your match score and missing keywords.
  4. Pick a template & download. Choose from 100+ resume templates (single-column ATS-safe and creative) and export free PDF, Word or PNG.

Why it's genuinely free

Most "free" resume builders make you pay to download or stamp a watermark on your file. ResumeForge doesn't. There's no paywall, no watermark and no account — build it, score it, tailor it and download it, completely free.
